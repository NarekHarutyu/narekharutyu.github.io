01

About

I'm a PhD student in Electrical Engineering and Computer Sciences at UC Berkeley, supported by the Berkeley Fellowship. I work where robot learning meets control: cross-embodiment visuomotor policies, reinforcement learning with certified stability, and sim-to-real transfer for legged, aerial, and mobile manipulators.

I earned my B.S. in Computer Engineering at Brown University, where I spent three years in Nora Ayanian's ACT Lab working on multi-robot systems and quadrotor swarms. I've twice been a Robotics Institute Summer Scholar at Carnegie Mellon, in Sebastian Scherer's AirLab and Guanya Shi's LeCAR Lab, and a SURF fellow at Caltech in Soon-Jo Chung's Aerospace Robotics and Control Lab.
